# AMCP Hardware

## Controller

- ESP32 Development Board

## Motor System

- TB6612FNG Dual Motor Driver
- DC Geared Motors
- Wheels

## Navigation Sensors

- IR Line Sensors

IR convention:

- White surface = 0
- Dark line / track = 1

## Obstacle Detection

- HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor

ESP32 requires a voltage divider on the HC-SR04 Echo output.

Suggested resistor configuration:

- 1 kΩ
- 2.2 kΩ

## Power

- Battery pack
- Appropriate regulated supply for ESP32 and motors

## Mechanical Platform

- Mobile chassis
- Modular cargo mounting area
